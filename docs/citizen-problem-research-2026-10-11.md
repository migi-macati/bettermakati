## Editorial doctrine
A problem-first pathway asks (1) Is anyone in immediate danger? (2) What concrete outcome is sought? (3) What evidence is safe/appropriate? (4) What entity controls the place, service or conduct? (5) What informal, administrative, social-work, enforcement or legal remedy exists? (6) How is the case tracked and escalated? A resident should never need to know an agency name to find the guide.

For emergency situations call **911** or appropriate Makati emergency hotline and do not wait for BetterMakati. For crimes/harassment, do not assert guilt or post identifying accusations. For homelessness, disability, poverty, minors, abuse or health, protect dignity, consent and privacy. Barangay mediation is not automatically appropriate, lawful or safe for all disputes; statutory exclusions and violence-sensitive protocols apply. Do not claim a complaint reached government without an actual referral receipt. Sources and locality have to be verified to the current barangay / roadway / property operator.

## Verification and publication pipeline
A citizen guide goes through `discovered -> agency_candidate -> law_reviewed -> route_verified -> locally_validated -> guide_ready -> published -> monitored`. Keep this editorial/route-verification state distinct from the repository publication state `draft -> ready -> branch_written -> pr_open -> ci_pending -> auto_merge_enabled -> merged -> deployed -> live_verified`.

Every published guide must include: (1) citizen-language issue statement/synonyms in English and accessible conversational Filipino/Taglish, (2) life-safety decision branch, (3) safe interim steps, (4) law/ordinance with authority and current applicability, (5) primary agency and exact verified official contact/report link, (6) alternate agency conditional on road, district, barangay, housing or service ownership, (7) evidence/privacy guidance, (8) a realistic action/result distinction without invented response-time promises, (9) alternate/escalation when no response, (10) checked date and source links, (11) related BetterMakati service, barangay, hotline, Civic Map, record or participation link. Do not require sign-in just to read. Never publicly map exact locations of victims/at-risk persons.

**Pilot priority:** CP-013 (neighbor karaoke), CP-025 (aggressive solicitation / threats), CP-037 (street parking). Prioritize frequent issues, severity, harm, Makati-specific legal clarity and jurisdiction ambiguity. Then expand across domains using query data and resident testing; do not assume frequency without evidence.

**Technical dependency to repair:** As of 11 Oct 2026, the existing `api/civic.js` stores cases in GitHub Issues of `migi-macati/bettermakati` by default. An authenticated attempt to create a repository Issue returned HTTP **410: Issues has been disabled in this repository.** This makes reliable native submission/tracking conditional on enabling Issues (if authorized) or changing to a functioning case store, with privacy review and end-to-end tests. Do not claim all forms work from frontend appearance alone. Existing `CivicNearbyReportForm.tsx` correctly distinguishes submission to BetterMakati from government referral; preserve the distinction.
