# BM-ORD-004 services directory density

**Owner:** Build & Fix  
**Date:** 30 October 2026 (Asia/Manila)  
**Route:** `/services`  
**Scope:** services directory only; accountability remains a separate batch.

## Reproduced problem

The services route rendered all 151 indexed services at once. Search and filters appeared in the hero, far above the result list, and their state was local to the page. Opening a guide and returning could lose the active search, filters, and expansion state. The page therefore required excessive scrolling and made it harder to understand which records the controls affected.

## Change

- Keep search, level, and category controls beside the service results.
- Render an initial 20 records and expose the remaining records in accessible 20-record batches.
- Filter and sort the complete canonical service dataset before taking the visible slice.
- Show an announced `Showing X of Y services` status.
- Persist `q`, `level`, `category`, and `shown` in the URL while preserving BetterBarangay scope.
- Keep canonical guide routes, source distinctions, record order, and service data unchanged.

## Acceptance evidence

The targeted browser regression checks assert:

- 20 of 151 records appear initially;
- keyboard activation loads the next 20;
- a record outside the first batch is discoverable through search;
- opening a guide and using Back restores the 40-record state;
- Filipino at 390px preserves language, keyboard operation, and horizontal fit.

Required build and browser-smoke CI remain authoritative for the exact pull-request head. Merge, matching READY production deployment, and live rendered checks are recorded separately in the shared handoff.
