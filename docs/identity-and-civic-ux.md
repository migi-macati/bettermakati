# BetterMakati identity and civic UX

Design pass: 20 September 2026. This records design decisions, not a new review date for underlying civic facts.

## Identity

The founder's Baybayin Ma sketch and Matagumpay hill-and-sun reference guide a contemporary independent civic mark. No assertion of standard Baybayin legibility or historically authenticated symbolism is made. Native SVG variants: full, monochrome, reversed, and simplified compact icon. The full sun uses radial eight-direction geometry; hill masking leaves its upper rays visible. The micro icon deliberately reduces detail and must not be presented as a literal historical sun.

Deep green #005447 is the identity and primary action colour. Gold #D5A62D is decorative, not body text on white. Wordmark uses regular Better and bold Makati. Avoid adding maps, buildings, flags or official seals. Keep clear space at least the height of the sun disc; avoid distortion. Human recognition testing with Baybayin readers and general residents is outstanding.

## Benchmark decisions

- Isomer https://www.isomer.gov.sg/ — consistent accessible responsive components, implemented through the shared shell, controls and typography.
- GOV.UK Design System https://design-system.service.gov.uk/ — predictable interaction patterns; task/search hierarchy and explicit input labels.
- gov.sg https://www.gov.sg/ — publisher identity and plain-language information. BetterMakati clearly remains independent, never displaying an official-government identity badge.
- Open Government Products https://www.open.gov.sg/ — report cards and transparent limitations; retain /status and link it from every content page.
- data.gov.sg https://data.gov.sg/ — inspectable data and reusable downloads; preserve chart/table controls, CSV and source links, and clarify filter scope and ranks.
- W3C https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html — 4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text. This design pass is not certification of full WCAG conformance.

## Page families

- Home: search, examples and common services before photographic discovery; keep civic records and comparisons accessible.
- Places/barangays: use location-matched licensed real photography only. Existing photographs retain captions, source and licence. Do not reuse a Poblacion image as a photograph of an unrelated barangay.
- History: contemporary landmark photos explicitly labelled; add archival material only when provenance, event relationship and reproduction rights are known. No generated historical evidence.
- Officials/elections: uniform neutral layout with sourced names, office, ballot year and results. No invented portrait or decorative candidate preference. Verified reusable portraits remain a content gap.
- Statistics: maintain period, unit, geography and source; filter labels describe this dataset, not all Philippine LGUs. Rankings remain national within the published top-ten comparison.
- All content: consistent correction link prefills the current path; sources and platform status are directly accessible. A review date is not a verification badge.

## Remaining human/content work

Baybayin recognition review, consented usability testing (including seniors and screen-reader users), licensed portraits, and event-matched archival imagery remain outstanding. These cannot be replaced with fabricated images, historical claims or automated accessibility scores. Automatic source discovery remains separate from editorial verification of civic claims.
