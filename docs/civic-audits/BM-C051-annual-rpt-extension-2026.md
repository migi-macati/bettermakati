## Public-interest finding

The canonical guide currently says Makati's **10% full-year early-payment discount had a January 20, 2026 deadline**. This accurately reflects the City's **January 11** initial announcement but omits the subsequently announced extension to **February 2, 2026**. This is a historical-date accuracy issue. It does **not** extend the separate **October 20, 2026** fourth-quarter **5%** prompt-payment deadline.
