# W7-6 — Editorial, history and report treatment

Status: active-bounded  
Reviewed: 2026-09-30

## Purpose

Polish BetterMakati's evidence-rich editorial surfaces without changing their civic claims, source relationships or information architecture.

W7-6 is divided so reports, history and heritage can each be implemented and verified as coherent slices rather than one oversized visual change.

## Sub-slices

### W7-6a — Reports & Insights

Polish the Reports landing page, report teasers and report article reading treatment. Preserve inline evidence, canonical/public-record handoffs, methodology, tables/charts and related civic records.

### W7-6b — History

Polish the Makati history timeline and long-form historical reading flow. Improve chronology, image/caption/source presentation and reading rhythm without adding or rewriting historical claims.

### W7-6c — Heritage

Polish heritage cards, collections, routes, captions/source links and the handoff between heritage places, the civic map and history. Preserve place-registry ownership and the distinction between places and walking routes.

## Shared rules

- No unsupported civic or historical claims.
- Sources and inline evidence stay close to the claims they support.
- Existing image credits, licenses and captions remain visible.
- Use the Wave 7 surface, spacing, green/gold and reading-width vocabulary.
- Preserve Wave 6 keyboard, responsive, reduced-motion and 44px interaction protections.
- Do not turn the pages into a repository, research notebook or decorative magazine.

## Current

W7-6a is implemented in the same commit as this plan and hands off to CI before W7-6b begins.
