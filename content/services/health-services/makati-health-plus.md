# Makati Health Plus / Yellow Card

The **Makati Health Plus Program** uses the Yellow Card for eligible beneficiaries and dependents.

The official application form asks for residency and household information and lists supporting documents such as a COMELEC certification or verification, barangay clearance, civil-registry documents for qualified dependents, and a PhilHealth Member Data Record, with additional documents depending on the applicant's situation.

Because eligibility and documentary rules can change, BetterMakati does not reproduce the full checklist here.
