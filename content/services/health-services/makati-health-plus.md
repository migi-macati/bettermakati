# Makati Health Plus / Yellow Card

The **Makati Health Plus Program** uses the Yellow Card for eligible beneficiaries and dependents.

The City of Makati's [Makati Health Plus application form](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/online_forms/pdf/401/MHP%20APPLICATION%20FORM%20%281%29.pdf) asks for residency and household information and lists supporting documents such as COMELEC certification or verification, barangay clearance, civil-registry documents for qualified dependents, and a PhilHealth Member Data Record.

Requirements vary depending on the applicant's situation.

## Where to start

1. Open the official [Makati Health Plus application form](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/online_forms/pdf/401/MHP%20APPLICATION%20FORM%20%281%29.pdf).
2. Check which eligibility and dependent rules apply to your household.
3. Prepare only the supporting records required for your situation.
4. Confirm the current submission channel with the City of Makati before sending personal documents.

## Before you submit

Because health-benefit eligibility and documentary requirements can change, use the current city form as the controlling checklist. BetterMakati does not ask you to upload IDs, medical records or application documents here.
