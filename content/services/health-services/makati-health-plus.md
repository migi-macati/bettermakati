# Makati Health Plus / Yellow Card

The **Makati Health Plus Program** issues the Yellow Card for subsidized healthcare services at Ospital ng Makati, Makati barangay health centers and lying-in clinics.

## Who may apply

The city's Makati Health Plus guide lists seven applicant categories:

1. Registered Makati voters who are actual Makati residents.
2. Registered Makati voters who live outside Makati.
3. Makati City Government employees.
4. National government employees assigned to the City Government of Makati.
5. Retired Makati City Government employees.
6. Relocated residents included in the Makati Social Welfare Department database.
7. Residential care facilities endorsed by MSWD.

## Requirements

Use the checklist for your applicant and card type. The official guide separates requirements for Solo, Family, Senior Citizen, Permanent Senior, Makati City Government or national-government employee, retired employee, relocatee and residential-care-facility applications.

Common or applicant-dependent records include:

- Voter's Certificate for voter-based categories.
- Updated PhilHealth Member Data Record. Family-card applicants should have qualified beneficiaries reflected in the MDR.
- PhilHealth payment receipt, employment certificate or latest payslip when required by the applicant's PhilHealth category.
- Certificate of Live Birth for new applicants, Marriage Certificate if married, and Barangay Certificate.
- Birth certificates and, when applicable, Makati school IDs for dependents under a Family Card.
- White Card or Senior Citizen Card and Makatizen Card for senior applicants.
- Land-title certification and an updated real-property-tax receipt in the applicant's name for the applicable Permanent Senior card type.
- Service Record for retired city employees.
- Certificate of Employment and employee ID, if available, for Makati City Government or assigned national-government employees.
- Relocation Certificate or MSWD endorsement for applicable applicants.

Read the [official Makati Health Plus guide](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/staticmenu/docs/1715048169416.pdf) and use the [Makati Health Plus application form](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/online_forms/pdf/401/MHP%20APPLICATION%20FORM%20%281%29.pdf) for the full applicant-specific checklist.

## Where to submit

Submit the application and requirements through the Makati Health Plus / Makati Action Center workflow.

**Makati Health Plus Program Office**  
Ground Floor, New Building  
Makati City Hall, J.P. Rizal Street  
Poblacion, Makati City

The official program guide lists **(02) 8870-1658** and **(02) 8870-1659** for inquiries and follow-ups. Makati's separate [current hotlines page](https://www.makati.gov.ph/content/makati-hotlines) lists **(02) 8899-8930** for Yellow Card applications. The city sources do not state that one listing supersedes the other.

## Fees and processing

The program guide lists **no fee** for the application, review and release stages.

Its published processing components are:

- 30 minutes for review and assessment.
- 1 day for preparation, printing and approval.
- 15 minutes for endorsement to the Makati Action Center.
- 1 day for release.

These are stage times, not a guaranteed end-to-end turnaround. Home visitation, incomplete or illegible requirements, and queueing may add time.
