# Get Emergency Assistance

For an immediate emergency, dial **911**. The Philippines' Unified 911 service is free and available 24/7 for police, fire, medical, rescue and other emergencies.

The Makati DRRMO Citizen's Charter describes local emergency response for medical emergencies, disasters, crime, vehicular accidents and other incidents in Makati.

## Information to prepare

When reporting an incident, be ready to provide:

- type of incident or assistance needed;
- exact location and a nearby landmark;
- contact information; and
- other details requested by the call taker.
