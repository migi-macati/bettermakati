# Get Emergency Assistance

For an immediate emergency, dial **911**. The [national emergency hotline directory](https://ehotlines.e.gov.ph/) covers police, fire, medical, rescue and other emergencies.

The [Makati Citizen's Charter](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/downloads/2/481/pdf/Makati%20Citizen%27s%20Charter%20-%20FOR%20PRINT.pdf) describes local emergency response for medical emergencies, disasters, crime, vehicular accidents and other incidents in Makati.

## Information to prepare

- type of incident or assistance needed;
- exact location and a nearby landmark;
- contact information; and
- other details requested by the call taker.
