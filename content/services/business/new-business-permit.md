# Apply for a New Business Permit

A business operating in Makati needs a city business permit. The **Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)** handles applications.

## Basic requirements

The current official business permit application form lists, among other requirements for a new application:

- accomplished business permit application form;
- locational clearance and barangay clearance;
- proof of business-name registration through DTI, SEC or CDA, as applicable;
- lease documents if the premises are rented, or title/tax declaration if owned;
- Comprehensive General Liability insurance, subject to stated exemptions; and
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate for business before permit issuance.

Requirements can vary by business type. Use the official form as the controlling reference.
