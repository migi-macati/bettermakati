# Apply for a New Business Permit

The **Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)** handles business permit applications in Makati.

## Requirements

The City of Makati's [Business Permit Application Form](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/online_forms/pdf/522/business%20permit%20form%202025%20update1111.pdf) lists:

- accomplished business permit application form;
- locational clearance and barangay clearance;
- proof of business-name registration through DTI, SEC or CDA, as applicable;
- lease documents if the premises are rented, or title/tax declaration if owned;
- Comprehensive General Liability insurance, subject to stated exemptions; and
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate before permit issuance.

Additional requirements may apply depending on the business.

## Where to start

1. Download the current [Business Permit Application Form](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/online_forms/pdf/522/business%20permit%20form%202025%20update1111.pdf).
2. Prepare the ownership or lease, business-registration, barangay and locational documents that apply to the establishment.
3. Follow the current BPLO submission and assessment instructions on the City of Makati's official channels.
4. Complete any required fire-safety inspection before permit issuance.

## Fees and processing time

BetterMakati does not publish a fixed fee or processing time here because the assessment can depend on the business and current city rules. Use the official assessment and receipt for the amount due.
