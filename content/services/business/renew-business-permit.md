# Renew a Business Permit

The City of Makati's [Business Permit Application Form](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/online_forms/pdf/522/business%20permit%20form%202025%20update1111.pdf) covers business permit renewals.

## Requirements

- accomplished and signed application form;
- previous year's Mayor's Business Permit, billing assessment or official receipt;
- Income Tax Return and financial statements for the preceding year filed with the BIR;
- Comprehensive General Liability insurance, subject to stated exemptions; and
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate before permit issuance.

Additional requirements may apply depending on the business.
