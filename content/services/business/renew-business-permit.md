# Renew a Business Permit

The City of Makati's [Business Permit Application Form](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/online_forms/pdf/522/business%20permit%20form%202025%20update1111.pdf) covers business permit renewals.

## Requirements

- accomplished and signed application form;
- previous year's Mayor's Business Permit, billing assessment or official receipt;
- Income Tax Return and financial statements for the preceding year filed with the BIR;
- Comprehensive General Liability insurance, subject to stated exemptions; and
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate before permit issuance.

Additional requirements may apply depending on the business.

## Renewal workflow

1. Open the current [Business Permit Application Form](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/online_forms/pdf/522/business%20permit%20form%202025%20update1111.pdf).
2. Prepare the previous permit or assessment record and the preceding year's tax and financial documents listed by the city.
3. Follow the current BPLO renewal, assessment and payment instructions.
4. Complete any required fire-safety inspection before renewed permit issuance.

## Fees and deadlines

The amount due and the applicable renewal timetable are controlled by current city rules and the official assessment. Confirm both through the City of Makati before acting.
