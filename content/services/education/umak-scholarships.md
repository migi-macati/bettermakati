# University of Makati Scholarships and Grants

UMak states that incoming undergraduate students receive an **Academic Scholarship** with tuition exemption upon admission. Other achievement, special and sponsored scholarships are available subject to their own rules.

For Achievement and Special Scholarships, the current published process uses the student's **OLEA** account and online submission of requirements.
