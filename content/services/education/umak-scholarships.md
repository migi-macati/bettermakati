# University of Makati Scholarships and Grants

UMak's [Scholarships and Grants](https://www.umak.edu.ph/admissions/scholarships/) page states that incoming undergraduate students receive an **Academic Scholarship** with tuition exemption upon admission. Other achievement, special and sponsored scholarships are subject to their own rules.

For Achievement and Special Scholarships, the published process uses the student's **OLEA** account and online submission of requirements. See the [scholarship guidelines](https://www.umak.edu.ph/admissions/scholarships/scholarship-guidelines/) for details.
