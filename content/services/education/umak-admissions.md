# Apply to the University of Makati

The **University of Makati (UMak)** is a locally funded university owned by the City Government of Makati.

UMak publishes its [admissions process](https://www.umak.edu.ph/admissions/), [college admission requirements](https://www.umak.edu.ph/admissions/apply-college/admission-requirements/) and [admission results](https://www.umak.edu.ph/admissions/admissions-results/) on its official website.

Requirements vary by applicant type and academic program.
