# Apply to the University of Makati

The **University of Makati (UMak)** is a locally funded university owned by the City Government of Makati.

UMak publishes its current admissions process, program requirements, examination results and application updates on its official website. Requirements vary by applicant type and academic program.

For Academic Year 2026–2027, UMak's admissions pages include current application and qualification information.
