# Pay Real Property Tax

The **Real Property Tax Division** handles real property tax payments.

Its [Citizen's Charter](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/downloads/2/481/pdf/Real%20Property%20Tax%20Division.pdf) identifies property owners, administrators, authorized representatives and lessees as persons who may transact.

## Requirements

- previous realty-tax official receipts;
- Notice of Assessment; and
- Tax Declaration.

## Before paying

Have the property records listed in the Citizen's Charter available so the Real Property Tax Division can identify the correct account and assessment.

## 2026 payment discounts and land-tax relief

For **qualifying non-delinquent taxpayers**, Makati's published schedule offers a **5% discount for prompt quarterly real property tax payments**. The **fourth-quarter deadline is October 20, 2026**. **The 5% discount requires timely payment of both basic real property tax and the additional Special Education Fund (SEF) tax; payment of only one component does not qualify.** This condition appears in Section 2A.11 of the Makati Revised Revenue Code, reproduced in the [City Treasurer's official notice](https://blgf.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Makati-City-1.pdf). The earlier quarterly deadlines were January 20, April 20, and July 20. The City's separate **10% full-year early-payment discount** had a January 20, 2026 deadline; it is not an October offer.

Under **City Ordinance 2025-A-040**, the City announced **15% relief on land taxes** through 2027, retroactive to January 1, 2025. The City's announcement expressly excludes **quarterly RPT payments** and taxpayers who used the amnesty under **Ordinance 2025-042** from that 15% relief. The relief concerns the land-tax component; do not assume a 15% reduction applies to the entire property tax bill.

Source: [Makati City Government announcement, January 11, 2026](https://www.makati.gov.ph/content/news/132657). Confirm eligibility and the actual assessment with the Real Property Tax Division before paying.

## Amount due

BetterMakati does not calculate a taxpayer's liability. Use the City's current assessment and official receipt as the controlling amount, and verify any discount, penalty or payment deadline with the official city channel.
