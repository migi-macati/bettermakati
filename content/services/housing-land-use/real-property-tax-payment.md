# Pay Real Property Tax

The **Real Property Tax Division** handles real property tax payments.

The published Citizen's Charter identifies property owners, administrators, authorized representatives and lessees as persons who may transact.

## Basic documents listed in the charter

- previous realty-tax official receipts;
- Notice of Assessment; and
- Tax Declaration.

## Official source

[City of Makati — Real Property Tax Division Citizen's Charter](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/downloads/2/481/pdf/Real%20Property%20Tax%20Division.pdf)

> Confirm current payment channels, rates, discounts and documentary requirements with the City Government before transacting.
