# Pay Real Property Tax

The **Real Property Tax Division** handles real property tax payments.

Its [Citizen's Charter](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/downloads/2/481/pdf/Real%20Property%20Tax%20Division.pdf) identifies property owners, administrators, authorized representatives and lessees as persons who may transact.

## Requirements

- previous realty-tax official receipts;
- Notice of Assessment; and
- Tax Declaration.

## Before paying

Have the property records listed in the Citizen's Charter available so the Real Property Tax Division can identify the correct account and assessment.

## 2026 payment discounts, installment dates and land-tax relief

**October 20, 2026 was the announced cutoff for the 5% fourth-quarter prompt-payment discount**, not necessarily the last day to pay the fourth installment without penalty. The [Makati Revenue Code currently posted on the City's eBOSS website](https://makati-eboss.ph/files/references/CHAPTER%20II.%20REAL%20PROPERTY%20TAXES.pdf), Section 2A.10, lists **December 31** as the ordinary fourth-installment payment date without interest or penalty. The [City Treasurer's 2024 notice](https://blgf.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Makati-City-1.pdf) separately confirms that distinction for **2024**, not 2026. The Bureau of Local Government Finance also lists a [Makati City calendar-year 2026 RPT notice](https://blgf.gov.ph/notices-of-payment-of-real-property-tax-and-special-education-fund-tax-of-lgus-for-cy-2026/), but its linked document has not yet been inspected. Confirm the current 2026 installment date and any later amendments with the Real Property Tax Division before relying on the displayed Code.

For **qualifying non-delinquent taxpayers**, the City announced a **5% discount for prompt quarterly real property tax payments**. The fourth-quarter discount cutoff was October 20; the earlier announced cutoffs were January 20, April 20 and July 20. To qualify, both the basic real property tax and additional Special Education Fund (SEF) tax must be paid on time; paying only one does not qualify. These conditions appear in Section 2A.11 of the displayed Code.

**Sa madaling sabi:** Ang **October 20** ay cutoff ng **5% quarterly discount**, hindi awtomatikong huling araw para bayaran ang ikaapat na installment nang walang penalty. **December 31** ang ordinaryong petsang nakasaad sa Revenue Code na naka-post sa eBOSS, pero kailangan pang kumpirmahin sa City Treasurer kung may 2026 amendment. Huwag ipagpalagay na may 5% discount pa pagkatapos ng October 20.

The original **10% full-year early-payment discount** cutoff was January 20, 2026. According to January 21–22 contemporary reporting of a later City announcement, that discount was **extended through February 2, 2026**. The original signed text of Ordinance 2026-002 has not been independently inspected. This was an extension of the **January** discount, not a new October offer.

Under **City Ordinance 2025-A-040**, the City announced **15% relief on the land-tax component** through 2027, retroactive to January 1, 2025. The City's announcement excludes **quarterly RPT payments** and taxpayers who used amnesty under **Ordinance 2025-042** from that relief. Do not assume a 15% reduction applies to the entire property-tax bill.

**Correction (October 27, 2026):** The earlier guide called October 20 the fourth-quarter deadline without distinguishing the 5% discount cutoff from the ordinary installment date. This update makes that distinction and adds the reported February 2 extension of the January annual-discount offer. The original publication date is unchanged.

## Amount due

BetterMakati does not calculate a taxpayer's liability. Use the City's current assessment and official receipt as the controlling amount, and verify any discount, penalty or payment deadline with the official city channel.
