# Contact the Makati Action Center

The **Makati Action Center (MAC)** receives citizen concerns, feedback and complaints and coordinates with concerned city units. Its [Citizen's Charter](https://www.makati.gov.ph/assets/uploads/downloads/2/481/pdf/Makati%20Action%20Center.pdf) lists the following contact points:

- City Hall trunkline / general MAC concerns: **8870-1000**
- District I monitoring: **8870-1432**
- District II monitoring: **8870-1401**
- Community and Patient Relations Unit: **8899-8948**
- Office: 8/F, Makati City Hall Building II
