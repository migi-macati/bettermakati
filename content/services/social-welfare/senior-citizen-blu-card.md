# Senior Citizen Blu Card Services

Makati has long operated a **Blu Card** program for senior citizens through the Makati Social Welfare Department.

BetterMakati found an official Citizen's Charter document that describes eligibility, documentary requirements and the application flow, but that document is older. For that reason, v1.0 does **not** reproduce its detailed requirements as if they were current.

## What to do

Contact the Makati Social Welfare Department or City Hall through the official Makati portal to confirm the current Blu Card rules before applying.
